Security Deposit Demand Letter Template

Template — customize the fields in brackets
[Your Full Name] [Your Current Address] [City, State, ZIP] [Your Email] | [Your Phone] [Date] [Landlord Name] [Landlord Address] [City, State, ZIP] Subject: Demand for Return of Security Deposit Dear [Landlord Name], I am writing to formally demand the return of my security deposit of $[Amount] for the property located at [Rental Address], which I vacated on [Move-Out Date]. Under [State] law, landlords are required to return security deposits within [number] days of a tenant vacating the property, along with an itemized statement of any deductions. As of today, [number] days have passed since my move-out date and I have not received my deposit or any itemized statement. The property was left in clean condition, consistent with normal wear and tear. I have documentation including [photos/move-out checklist/etc.] confirming the condition of the property at the time of my departure. I demand the full return of $[Amount] within [14] days of this letter. If you believe deductions are warranted, please provide an itemized statement with supporting documentation. Failure to comply will result in legal action, which may include filing a claim in small claims court and seeking additional damages as permitted by [State] law. Sincerely, [Your Signature] [Your Printed Name] [Date]

If a refund is not issued by [Deadline], I will pursue the matter through small claims court and report this issue to the [Better Business Bureau / Federal Trade Commission / state consumer protection agency]. I look forward to your prompt resolution of this matter. Sincerely, [Your Signature] [Your Printed Name] [Date]
