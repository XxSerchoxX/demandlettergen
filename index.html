Frequently asked questions

  

    
What is a demand letter?
A demand letter is a formal written notice requesting that someone fulfill a legal obligation — typically paying a debt or issuing a refund — before you take legal action. Courts often require proof of a good-faith attempt to resolve a dispute before filing a lawsuit.

    
Is this tool really free?
Yes, completely free. No signup, no credit card, no hidden fees.

    
Do I need a lawyer to send a demand letter?
No. Anyone can write and send a demand letter. For complex disputes or large amounts, consulting an attorney is advisable.

    
How should I send the letter?
Send via certified mail with return receipt requested. This creates a documented record that the recipient received it — important if you need to go to court.

    
What if they ignore my letter?
Your next options include filing in small claims court (typically for amounts under $10,000–$15,000 depending on your state), mediation, or consulting an attorney about civil litigation.
