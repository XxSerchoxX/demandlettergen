Frequently asked questions

  

    

      
What is a demand letter?

      
A demand letter is a formal written notice sent to an individual or business requesting that they fulfill a legal obligation — typically paying a debt, issuing a refund, or repairing damages — before you escalate to legal action. Courts often require proof that you made a good-faith attempt to resolve the dispute before filing a lawsuit.

Do I need a lawyer to send a demand letter?

      
No. Anyone can send a demand letter — you do not need to be a lawyer or hire one. However, for high-value disputes or complex legal matters, consulting an attorney is always advisable.

    

    

      
How should I send the letter?

      
For the strongest legal standing, send the letter via certified mail with return receipt requested. This creates a documented record that the recipient received it. Email can also work for less formal disputes.

    

    

      
What if the recipient ignores my letter?

      
If the deadline passes without a response, your next options include filing in small claims court (for amounts typically under $10,000–$15,000 depending on your state), mediation, or consulting an attorney about civil litigation.
