Your Rights as a Tenant

  
In most US states, landlords are legally required to return your security deposit within 14 to 30 days of move-out, along with an itemized list of any deductions. Failure to do so can entitle you to additional damages — sometimes double or triple the deposit amount.

What to Do If the Landlord Doesn't Respond

  
If your landlord ignores your demand letter, your next step is small claims court. Most states allow tenants to sue for the deposit plus additional penalties. The demand letter you generate here will serve as evidence that you made a good-faith attempt to resolve the matter.


  

    
Tip: Send your demand letter via certified mail with return receipt. This gives you legal proof that the landlord received it.
